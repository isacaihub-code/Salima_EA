# Gold Standard — Preferred Copy Style

This example defines the quality bar for all external writing. Match this tone, clarity, and structure.

---

"We help SMEs save time and increase efficiency by automating their daily operations with AI.
Instead of juggling tools and repetitive tasks, our systems handle client onboarding, follow-ups, and internal workflows — so you can focus on growing your business."

---

## What makes this work

Direct and outcome-first — leads with the benefit, not the technology. No jargon. Warm but not fluffy. Specific enough to be credible, simple enough to be understood by any SME owner.

External writing should always clear this bar.
