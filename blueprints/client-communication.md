# Blueprint: Client Communication + Email Drafting

**Goal:** Draft professional client emails for Salima to review, approve, and send. Never send directly.
**Equipment:** None — this is native Architect work.
**Output:** A ready-to-send draft, presented inline for approval.

---

## When to Use This Blueprint

Trigger this workflow any time the request involves:
- Cold outreach to an SME prospect
- Follow-up after no reply
- Responding to an inbound enquiry
- Sending a proposal or quote summary
- Onboarding a new client
- Replying to a frequently asked question
- Invoice or payment follow-up
- Any other external message to a client or prospect

---

## Required Inputs

Before drafting, confirm you have:

1. **Email type** — which of the types above applies?
2. **Recipient context** — who is this person? What do they do? Where are they in the pipeline?
3. **Objective** — what should this email achieve? (book a call, send a document, follow up, etc.)
4. **Tone notes** — any specific context that changes the default tone? (e.g. warm relationship, first contact, sensitive topic)
5. **Any prior exchange** — paste or summarise what's been said before, if relevant

If any of these are missing, stop and ask. Do not draft on incomplete context.

---

## Draft Process

1. **Orient** — restate the objective in one sentence before writing
2. **Draft** — write the email following the tone rules below
3. **Review against checklist** — check every item before presenting
4. **Present** — show the draft clearly labelled, with a one-line summary of what it does

---

## Tone Rules (External)

Professional, clear, and warm. Outcome-first — lead with what matters to the reader, not to us.

- No jargon, no filler, no corporate speak
- Short paragraphs — 2-3 sentences max per block
- One clear ask per email — never more
- Subject line: specific and useful, not generic

**Gold standard benchmark:** references/goldstandard/preferred-copy-style.md

---

## Email Types — Quick Reference

| Type | Lead with | Close with |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Cold outreach | Their problem, not our product | A single low-friction ask (call, reply, question) |
| Follow-up (no reply) | Brief context, no guilt | A simple yes/no or one-click ask |
| Inbound reply | Acknowledgement + answer | Next step clearly stated |
| Proposal summary | What they get, not what we do | Call to action with deadline or timeframe |
| Onboarding welcome | Reassurance + what happens next | First action they need to take |
| FAQ reply | Direct answer first | Offer to elaborate if needed |
| Invoice follow-up | Friendly, factual, no apology | Clear payment details and deadline |

---

## Output Format

Present the draft like this:

---
**Draft — [Email Type] — [Recipient name or description]**

**To:** [recipient]
**Subject:** [subject line]

[body]

---

*Ready to send? Say "approve" or tell me what to change.*

---

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- **Never send.** Every output is a draft. Salima approves before anything goes out.
- If the email is sensitive (complaint, negotiation, first contact with a high-value prospect), flag it: "This one warrants a careful read before sending."
- If inputs are missing, ask — do not invent context.
- If Salima asks for a revision, update the draft and present the full revised version, not just the changed section.
