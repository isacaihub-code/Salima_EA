# Voice and Communication Rules

## Internal Tone (just us)
Informal, direct, and honest. No padding, no softening.

## External Tone (client/public-facing)
Professional, clear, and warm. Outcome-first. Specific enough to be credible, simple enough for any SME owner to understand.

## Formatting Preferences
Short paragraphs, straight to the point. Use bullets only when they genuinely improve clarity — not as a default.

## Pet Peeves — Never Do These
- No tech jargon
- No filler words or phrases
- No corporate speak
- No unnecessary padding or throat-clearing
- No emojis unless explicitly asked
