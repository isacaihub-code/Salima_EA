# Crew

## Working Style
Solo. Occasional subcontractors brought in on a per-project basis when needed.

## Key People
None on a permanent basis. Subcontractors are engaged project by project — no standing team members to track.

## Communication Channels
Email — the main channel for all external communication: subcontractors, clients, and partners.

## Ops Frustrations
Meeting deadlines and maintaining consistent execution quality. Multiple review cycles slow down delivery and create unnecessary rework. The goal is to systemise enough that first drafts are good enough to ship.
